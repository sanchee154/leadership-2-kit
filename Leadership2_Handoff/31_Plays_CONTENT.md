### Close one loop end to end
- **The move:** Run one small transaction all the way through before designing any system around it, for example one purchase, one receipt and the money back.
- **Script:** (none)
- **When:** Weekly
- **Cost:** One small purchase and one receipt
- **Source:** UR, Runs 1 and 8

### Door ask
- **The move:** Make one small ask to one person as they walk in. It's not a role list.
- **Script:** "Could you serve the ice cream?"
- **When:** Arrival
- **Cost:** One sentence
- **Source:** UR, Run 2, and YSA handoff

### Free hands
- **The move:** Have everything handled before people arrive, so that during the arrival window you're carrying nothing and minding nothing.
- **Script:** (none)
- **When:** Before
- **Cost:** Arriving early with the prep done
- **Source:** UR, Run 3

### Fixed-day announcement
- **The move:** Send the announcement on the same weekday every week, even before the details are final.
- **Script:** "Sunday, [time], [place]"
- **When:** Weekly
- **Cost:** One text and one recurring phone alarm
- **Source:** UR, Run 4

### Get the number
- **The move:** Ask every new face for their number before they leave, and type it in while they're still standing there.
- **Script:** "What's your number so I can text you about next week?"
- **When:** After
- **Cost:** Four seconds
- **Source:** UR, Run 5, and YSA handoff

### Feed the existing channel
- **The move:** Forward the regular information you already send to the person who runs a channel that already exists. Become their most reliable source.
- **Script:** (none)
- **When:** Weekly
- **Cost:** One forward a week
- **Source:** UR, Run 6

### Name the role out loud
- **The move:** Tell the person who has been quietly filling an unnamed role what they were doing, and that you didn't see it at the time. Attach no ask.
- **Script:** (none)
- **When:** After
- **Cost:** One conversation
- **Source:** UR, Run 7

### No-agenda call
- **The move:** Call one person for no reason. Don't bring up the group unless they do.
- **Script:** "Hey [name], it's Eduardo. No reason for calling, just realized I hadn't talked to you in a while. How've you been?" Then stop talking.
- **When:** Weekly
- **Cost:** One call
- **Source:** UR, Run 9, and YSA handoff

### One note a week
- **The move:** Write one handwritten note to one person each week. Add a second only after the first habit has held for a month.
- **Script:** (none)
- **When:** Weekly
- **Cost:** One note
- **Source:** UR, Run 10

### FROG conversation
- **The move:** Talk with someone about Family, Recreation, Occupation and Goals, and remember one fact to follow up on.
- **Script:** (none)
- **When:** During
- **Cost:** One conversation
- **Source:** YSA handoff (FROG method research, Maher)

### 48-hour follow-up
- **The move:** Follow up with every newcomer within 48 hours of their first visit.
- **Script:** (none)
- **When:** After
- **Cost:** One text or call per newcomer
- **Source:** Goals plan, weekly input 1
