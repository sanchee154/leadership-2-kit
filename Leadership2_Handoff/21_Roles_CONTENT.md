# Roles: Content

Copy each entry exactly. Every entry gets the same defaults:
- **Status:** Unnamed
- **Applies to:** Volunteer group, Enterprise team, Digital org
- **Holder and Backup:** left blank. Don't fill them in.

These five roles are the unnamed roles listed in Part 3 and Run 7 of "The Group Runs on Unnamed Roles" (UR).

---

### Food
- **What it covers:** Buying and providing food for each gathering.
- **Signal it sends:** Food is there when people arrive, and the receipt goes through the group's reimbursement path.
- **Source:** UR, Run 1 and Part 3

### Activity planning
- **What it covers:** Deciding what the group does at each gathering.
- **Signal it sends:** Each gathering has a planned activity.
- **Source:** UR, Run 7 and Part 3

### Communication
- **What it covers:** Sending the regular announcement and feeding information into channels that already exist.
- **Signal it sends:** An announcement goes out on the same day every week.
- **Source:** UR, Runs 4 and 6, and Part 3

### Contact capture
- **What it covers:** Getting contact information from new people before they leave.
- **Signal it sends:** Every new face is reachable the next week.
- **Source:** UR, Run 5 and Part 3

### Counting
- **What it covers:** Counting who comes.
- **Signal it sends:** (blank. The source names this role but doesn't describe it. Leave this field empty for Eduardo.)
- **Source:** UR, Run 7 ("Food, planning, counting")
